The picture: the public server is reception (I can reach it). The internal machines are back offices with no street door (I can't). I break into reception = a foothold, then tunnel THROUGH it so my tools reach the back offices. I never jump machines — I route through the one I own.
Decision rule:  Can I SSH into the target?  →  YES + one service -L  ·  YES + everything -D  ·  NO (shell only) -R  ·  no SSH at all → chisel

Phase 2 — Tunnel through the pivot
